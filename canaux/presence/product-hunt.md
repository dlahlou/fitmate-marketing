## Description (260 caractères maximum)

> Most of us want to move more. What stops us is rarely motivation: it's having no one to go with. Fitmates connects you with people near you, at your level, for hikes, runs and more. You come alone, you leave with a group. Free app; some outings are paid.

(254 caractères ; « some outings are paid » = certaines sorties sont payantes si l'organisateur le décide)
